About Me

A concise academic introduction. Replace or expand this section with the biography from your CV when the source document is available.

I am an Assistant Lecturer in the Department of Microbiology at Sokoto State University. My research interest is in environmental microbiology, an area that explores microorganisms in natural and human-impacted environments and their potential roles in environmental processes and applications.

This website is designed to provide students, researchers, collaborators and academic institutions with an accessible overview of my academic work, research interests and scholarly profiles.

Research Interests

Core areas presented from the information supplied for this website.

Environmental Microbiology

Microbial communities, environmental processes and the relationships between microorganisms and their surrounding environments.

Applied Microbiology

Exploring microbial knowledge and methods for practical environmental and scientific applications.

Microbial Ecology

Understanding microbial diversity, interactions and ecological roles in environmental systems.
